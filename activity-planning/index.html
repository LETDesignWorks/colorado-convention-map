Delegate & bus schedule

All times are planned Denver times, not live tracking. Delegates must be at the pickup point by the loading-start time. Loading is included in the stop duration; 0 loading minutes means no boarding is planned. Older routes default to 0 until you set loading minutes and save again.

Driving estimates do not include live traffic or motorcoach restrictions. Review loading curbs and bus access with the operator. Manual driving minutes replace the road estimate; the per-leg allowance is still added.
