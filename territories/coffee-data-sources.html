Coffee listing sources

Every new territory PDF selects up to five of the closest listed coffee shops and cafes to the center of that territory's homes, sorted by straight-line distance. There is no two-mile cutoff.

Within Colorado, listings come from Overture Maps Places, release 2026-09-23.1. This extract includes locations categorized as coffee shops with an existence confidence of at least 0.95, excluding records marked closed. The search uses the whole extract to find the nearest shops.

Outside this coverage, near its edges, or if the extract cannot load, the planner searches worldwide cafe listings from OpenStreetMap contributors, licensed under the Open Database License (ODbL), through the public VK Maps and Overpass API services. Searches widen from approximately 1.5 to 5, 20, 80, and 200 kilometers around the territory as needed, stopping when five closer listings have been found. Only the approximate search area is sent; home addresses, territory names, and avoid lists are not sent. Results are reused for 30 minutes in the current page.

Remote areas may have fewer mapped cafes. If online lookup fails, available Colorado listings are retained and the PDF explains the incomplete search. Every coffee page includes a link to search the area directly. Export a new PDF to refresh its coffee listings; previously saved PDFs retain their original contents.

Listings are a dated planning reference. Check current hours and availability before visiting. The data has been filtered, deduplicated, and simplified for the territory planner.
